# Needs-Led Search Targeting

Reviewed 16 October 2026 following the owner's request to prioritise buying
needs over individual model names. Danish copy is mirrored in English.
Existing model pages, their URLs and specifications remain available.

## Public Competitor Observations

These observations concern wording and page structure, not verified rankings,
keyword search volumes, advertising bids, traffic or conversion rates. The
research used Danish-language queries and the businesses' own public pages;
it is not a controlled, logged-out Google Denmark page-one comparison.

| Business                                                                    | Observed approach                                                                                                                                                                                                | Application at Kestro                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Intent And Existing Destinations

| Need                         | Danish target phrases                                                               | Existing destination                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choose a computer            | hvilken computer passer til mit behov                                               | `/produkter`, as a supporting choice question                                                |
| Choose a laptop              | hvilken baerbar skal jeg vaelge; hvilken baerbar til virksomheden                   | `/vejledninger/tjek-brugt-baerbar-foer-koeb`                                                 |
| Budget laptop                | billig baerbar til arbejde; billige baerbare computere til virksomheder             | `/produkter/baerbare-computere`; budget explanation in the buying guide                      |
| Company laptop               | firmabaerbar; erhvervsbaerbar                                                       | `/produkter/baerbare-computere`                                                              |
| Startup equipment            | baerbar til startup; baerbar til ivaerksaettere; it-udstyr til nystartet virksomhed | Laptop category and `/produkter`                                                             |
| Danish or Norwegian keyboard | baerbar med dansk tastatur; baerbar med norsk tastatur                              | Laptop category for buying; `/ydelser/nordisk-tilpasning` for physical preparation           |
| Upgrade an old laptop        | opgradering af gammel baerbar; RAM; SSD                                             | `/reparation/ram-og-ssd-opgradering`                                                         |
| Software update              | opdatering af baerbar                                                               | An explanatory FAQ distinguishes this from hardware; not a primary hardware-service keyword. |

The table uses ASCII spellings for documentation; published Danish uses the
actual Danish letters. The keyword CSV contains the natural Danish phrases.
Volume, difficulty and CPC remain unknown. Priority expresses implementation
relevance, not measured market size or a ranking guarantee.

## Implementation Boundaries

- Needs and startup use cases precede brands on the laptop category.
- No new URLs, price-band pages or parallel keyword-only guides.
- Brand names are removed from the laptop category title, not erased from
  accurate specifications or established model URLs.
- Prices remain existing dated external budget references, not Kestro offers.
- No new homepage-hero artwork, animation, dependencies or paid service.
- The buying guide retains its original publication date when updated.
