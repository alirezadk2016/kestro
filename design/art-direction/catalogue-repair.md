# Catalogue repair — 13 November 2026

## Reassessment

The owner found the retail adaptation uncomfortable. Earlier approval weighted
legibility and working controls too highly: 786px laptop cards, 7,552px of mobile
model-card height and six large missing-photo panels are failures of density.
Global photo-first ordering also splits a family across unrelated hardware.

Keep the confirmed brand/Elgiganten-inspired browsing direction, but correct its
product fit: Kestro lists model families and four historical budget references.
Do not imitate the density of a large configured-stock retailer. Existing owner
authorization covers design decisions, fixes and publication without another
approval checkpoint. Use tasteful-ui production implementation plus a full craft
review. Preserve metadata, H1s, links, factual content and honest media labels.

## Concrete correction

- Model families default to genuine compact rows: small complete-device image
  or identity tile, model link, three concise facts and one compact action area.
  Details remain on model pages and the comparison. No large empty photo stages.
- Optional model grid caps actual media height at 192–208px and uses a short
  identity band for a missing photo. No fake laptop image is generated.
- Canonical product-group order, media preference within each group only;
  stable facet order and explicit mobile-workstation label. Concise dock facts
  retain qualifications and full comparison specifications.
- Four budgets retain a balanced image grid. Brand/type controls open in a
  compact in-flow panel; search and historical-budget sort remain. No redundant
  permanent sidebar or view toggle for four references.
- Flatten nested toolbar panels and show reset only when there is a selection
  to clear. Keep real controls, 44px targets, readable captions and no-JS links.
- While comparing, remove the redundant floating tray and provide compact
  inline selection/removal. Close returns to the previous browsing position.

## Reproduced functional defects

1. Literal substring search rejects `Lenovo T480`, repeated spaces and budget
   queries such as `T14 16 GB`: normalize tokens and AND them across real text.
2. Fresh opening the generated `?type=workstations#workstations` leaves the
   selected model above the viewport: restore known valid fragments with or
   without the explicit type parameter after the filtered result commits. Drop
   a known category fragment when that category is removed from a mixed type
   selection, so a shared URL cannot retain a filtered-out anchor.
3. Mobile Shift+Tab hides focused model links beneath sticky comparison labels:
   ensure the complete focused target remains in the unobscured scroll area.
4. Removing the focused selection drops focus to body: move to the nearest
   remaining selection control or search, without a focus trap.
