## Direction and hierarchy

- Four equally framed product images, one column on a phone, two on a tablet,
  four on a wide desktop. Quiet category labels distinguish the two laptops,
  SFF computer and monitor. No filters for a four-item collection, no rotation,
  invented badges, discounts, stock controls or cart.
- The collection heading and a short visible dated/VAT/fee basis anchor all
  four cards. Keep the four historical amounts and 14 October read date.
  The site's project/prelaunch notice remains visible. Page H1, title,
  description, canonical, hreflang and URLs remain unchanged.
- Images are whole and centered in stable 4:3 frames with `object-contain`.
  First image prioritised; other assets lazy and locally served. Existing
  owner-supplied T480 front image and exact T14 Gen 2 Intel CC0 photo.
- No exact authorised Dell photos were found. Use original, visibly labelled
  schematic illustrations of the 5060 SFF form and U2419H form, respecting
  verified physical proportions. They are not photographs of seller units.
  Visible badges distinguish photo from illustration; a native credit fold
  provides T14 author/source/CC0 and explains the original Dell artwork.
- Keep the complete configuration readable. The main action asks about that
  exact configuration in the existing non-binding quote form. Model specs
  and dated external price source are quieter secondary destinations.
  The price source is historical; its current default variant may differ.
- Keep budgets on model pages visibly dated too. Preserve generational/SFF/
