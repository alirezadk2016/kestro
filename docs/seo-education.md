## Evidence and Boundaries

- Market article uses only TrendForce's free public summaries dated
  24 and 30 September 2026. The full reports were not purchased or read.
  Contract, spot and retail pricing are not interchangeable. No claim that
  every laptop rose in price; no invented percentage or future buying deadline.
- Lenovo PSREF supplies the T480, X1 Carbon Gen 7, P53 and T14 Gen 2 Intel
  examples. Listed/tested maxima are not universal electrical limits.
  Product generations, CPU generations and regional configurations differ.
- Microsoft supplies PC Health Check, Windows requirements, unsupported
  installation warnings, servicing and BitLocker recovery guidance.
- Dell supplies service and swollen-battery precautions. Every DIY job
  still needs the exact model's manual; no generic battery disconnection
  procedure, ten-minute promise or claim that ESD is the only risk.
- Buying and capacity recommendations are identified as practical judgement,
  not measured performance, customer experience or universal saving claims.
- The hub discloses AI assistance. Existing author information is retained;
  no invented qualifications, tests or testimonials are added.
- Sources are registered in `lib/sources.ts`. Publication dates are preserved
  when articles are updated, including the Atom feed.

## SEO Purpose

Answer pre-purchase and repair questions thoroughly, then link to the relevant
next action. The hub, topic groups, related articles and laptop category
provide crawlable links. New routes use both language mappings, reciprocal
hreflang, self-canonicals, the generated sitemap, Article schema, search
index and the Danish feed.

The 213 keyword-map rows are editorial targets, not proven demand. Search
volume, difficulty and CPC remain unknown. No traffic uplift or score is
promised. The existing 56 unindexed-page report cannot be resolved from its
aggregate screenshot; this content work is not proof those URLs are fixed.

## Measuring Results

After deployment, check Search Console indexing for the six new language URLs.
Compare impressions, query relevance, clicks and CTR by article and country
over complete periods. Separate newly published pages from refreshed pages
and branded searches from informational searches. Account for crawl delay
and low sample sizes; no immediate before/after ranking claim.

Evaluate useful enquiries separately from reading traffic, and only use
analytics collected under the site's existing consent rules. Refresh
market claims when evidence changes, not by changing a date without content.

Reference: [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
